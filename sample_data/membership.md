# BookNook membership

## Hours
The reading room is open Tuesday through Sunday, from ten in the morning until eight in the evening. Monday is shelving day and the room is closed to guests.

## Borrowing
A member may borrow four books at a time. The loan lasts twenty-one days. Renewals are handled at the front desk, not by post.

## Late fee
The late fee is one book credit per day after seven days past the due date. Credits are collected at the front desk and cannot be paid with cash.

## Guests
Each member may bring one guest on Sunday afternoon. Guests may sit in the window chairs. Guests do not borrow books.
