# Shelving guide

## Fiction
Mystery novels stay on aisle M in the rainy day corner. Cozy titles face the window so a reader can find them without asking.

## Returns
Returned books rest on the blue cart for a full day before they go back to the shelf. Do not reshelve a damp book.

## Oversized
Atlases and art folios lie flat on the oak table. They are not loaned overnight.
